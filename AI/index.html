Tadpol Slaughter

The Game

Why I Created This Game

My fly collection was eaten by frogs, so I can't let the tadpoles through to mature. This game lets me protect my flies by stopping tadpoles before they become full-grown frogs!

How to Play

Move the saw up and down to kill the tadpoles and protect your fly collection!
